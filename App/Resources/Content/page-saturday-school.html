IACS

Faith, identity, and community.

Established in the early 2000s by a dedicated group of families, IAC was born from a heartfelt desire: to ensure their children remained connected to the rich heritage, timeless values, and beautiful teachings of Islam. These founders, driven by love for their faith and concern for future generations, built more than just a school; they created a space where identity, community, and character could flourish together.

We Learn Hard, Play Hard, and Grow Together!

From Foam Parties, Petting Zoos, and Bouncy Houses to Quran Competitions, Ramadan Sleepovers, Fatemyeh Art Shows, Weekly Quran Circles, Arbaeen Around the World, and Ghadeer & Mid-Sha’ban celebrations, our school is where faith, fun, learning, and community come together!
