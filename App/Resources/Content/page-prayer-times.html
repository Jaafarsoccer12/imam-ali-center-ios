Calculation method and source

Springfield, Virginia coordinates are interpreted using AlAdhan’s TEHRAN method (method 7), which identifies the Institute of Geophysics, University of Tehran as the calculation method. The University’s Calendar Center publishes calendar and prayer-time resources. This is the University of Tehran method applied to Springfield; it is not a University-published Springfield timetable.
