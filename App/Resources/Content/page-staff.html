In Service of the Community

In service of Imam Mahdi (a.s.)

اللَّهُمَّ اجْعَلْنِي مِنْ أَنْصَارِهِ وَ أَعْوَانِهِ وَ الذَّابِّينَ عَنْهُ وَ الْمُسَارِعِينَ‏ فِي‏ حَوَائِجِهِ وَ الْمُمْتَثِلِينَ لِأَوَامِرِهِ وَ نَوَاهِيهِ وَ السَّابِقِينَ‏ إِلَى إِرَادَتِهِ وَ الْمُحَامِينَ عَنْهُ

"O Allah, make me among Imam Mahdi’s helpers, his supporters, his defenders, those who hasten to fulfill his needs, those who comply with his commands and prohibitions, those who precede others in fulfilling his will, and those who protect him."

At the heart of the Imam Ali Center is a dedicated family of staff and volunteers whose selfless commitment fuels every aspect of our community life. Volunteering in the service of Allah (swt) and His creation is a noble act of worship (‘Ibadah) that transforms individual effort into a lasting spiritual blessing. From organizing weekly programs and teaching in our weekend school to maintaining our facilities and welcoming visitors, our volunteers embody the compassionate spirit of the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him and his family) and the Ahlulbayt (peace be upon them). We extend our deepest gratitude to every individual who generously offers their time, talents, and energy; your contributions build a thriving, supportive home for all, and our center simply could not fulfill its mission without you.
