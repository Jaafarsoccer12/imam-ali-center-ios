Honoring Our Loved Ones with Dignity and Devotion

At the Imam Ali Center, we understand that losing a loved one is one of life’s most profound trials. During these moments of sorrow, our community is here to support your family with compassion, care, and full adherence to Islamic traditions. We take on the sacred responsibility of fulfilling all religious rites so you can focus on grief, remembrance, and prayer.

All of our funeral and bereavement services are conducted strictly in accordance with Ja'fari (Shia) jurisprudence (Fiqh), ensuring every step is executed with reverence and proper religious oversight.

FUNERAL & BEREAVEMENT

Our Funeral & Bereavement Services Include:

✦

Washing of the Deceased (Ghusl al-Mayyit)

Full ritual purification performed with complete dignity and respect by trained brothers and sisters, adhering strictly to Ja'fari Fiqh regulations (Ghusl, Kafan shrouding, and Hunut).

☾

Funeral Prayer (Salat al-Janazah)

Arranging and officiating the mandatory congregational prayer for the deceased led by qualified resident scholars.

⌖

Burial & Cemetery Services

Coordination of transportation, cemetery arrangements, and the final burial rites (Talqin and interment) according to proper Islamic rulings.

⌂

Memorial & Fatiha Hall Rental

On-site rental of our spacious hall for Majalis (memorial gatherings), Majlis-e-Aza, Fatiha recitations, and receiving condolences. We provide full facility support for your family and guests.
