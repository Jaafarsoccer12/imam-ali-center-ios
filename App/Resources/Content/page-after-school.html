A Place to Learn, Grow & Shine!

At the Imam Ali Center After School Program, every afternoon is an opportunity to learn something new, build meaningful friendships, and grow in faith! We provide a safe, welcoming, and nurturing environment where elementary and middle school students can thrive academically, spiritually, and socially.

Our program combines homework support and academic enrichment with engaging Quranic learning, Islamic values, and character-building activities. Through interactive experiences, positive mentorship, and a strong sense of community, we help students grow in confidence, strengthen their Islamic identity, and develop the values that will guide them throughout their lives.

We believe after-school hours should be more than just time spent waiting for the day to end. They should be filled with purpose, discovery, friendship, and fun! Parents can enjoy peace of mind knowing their children are in a structured, uplifting Islamic environment where they are encouraged to reach their potential, build strong character, and shine both inside and outside the classroom.

Join us for afternoons filled with learning, laughter, faith, and growth, where every child belongs and every child has the opportunity to shine!
