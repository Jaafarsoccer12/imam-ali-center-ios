Resident Scholar (Alim)

Sheikh Mustafa Akhound

Sheikh Mustafa Akhound serves as the Resident Scholar (Alim) at the Imam Ali Center, acting as the center's active spiritual director and safeguarding its core founding mission. As the local resident authority, he leads daily and Friday (Jumu'ah) prayers, delivers regular sermons, and officiates major religious observances, including Ramadan and Eid programs. Grounded in traditional seminary leadership, Sheikh Mustafa provides compassionate pastoral care—offering family counseling, officiating marriage ceremonies (Nikah), and guiding youth through dedicated spiritual mentorship. In his leadership role, he oversees all institutional partnerships and public programs to ensure the center steadfastly preserves its core values while serving the broader Northern Virginia community.

Assistant Resident Scholar

Sheikh Mohammad Baghernejad

Sheikh Mohammad Baghernejad serves as Assistant Resident Scholar at the Imam Ali Center in Springfield, Virginia, where he shapes the academic vision and leads the institutional curriculum.
