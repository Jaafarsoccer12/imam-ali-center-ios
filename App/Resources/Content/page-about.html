NONPROFIT & CHARITABLE SERVICE

Imam Ali Center is a nonprofit charitable organization established by Imam Shirazi World Foundation, a 501(c)(3) organization. Our work supports worship, education, and charitable service in Northern Virginia.

Our Mission

At the Imam Ali Center, our mission is to serve, educate, and empower our community by fostering a vibrant spiritual environment grounded in the Holy Qur’an and the teachings of the Holy Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him and his family) and his blessed household, the Ahlulbayt (peace be upon them). We are dedicated to providing comprehensive religious, educational, and social services that nurture moral integrity, strengthen faith, and promote compassionate service to all people, regardless of background, race, or ethnicity.

Our Vision

We envision a unified, enlightened, and resilient community where Islamic values inspire personal growth, family strength, and civic responsibility. Our primary focus is to empower the next generation—nurturing our youth to become compassionate leaders, confident in their Islamic identity and actively contributing to the betterment of society.

Our Core Values

Faith & Knowledge (Iman & 'Ilm)

Anchoring our lives in authentic Islamic scholarship, spiritual reflection, and continuous learning.

Inclusivity & Community First

Welcoming all individuals and families into a supportive, open, and dignified sanctuary.

Youth Empowerment

Investing heavily in youth development, mentorship, and educational initiatives to build a strong foundation for future leaders.

Compassionate Service

Serving humanity with humility, respect, and generosity as guided by the example of Imam Ali (peace be upon him).

Integrity & Mission Guardianship

Preserving our sacred traditions while promoting peaceful coexistence, dialogue, and positive engagement within our local community.

Our History

Established in Northern Virginia, the Imam Ali Center was founded as an independent, non-profit organization through the dedication of local believers seeking a permanent spiritual home. Created to fulfill the growing needs of the Shia Muslim community in the Washington, D.C. metropolitan area, the Center has evolved from a modest gathering place into a full-service Islamic institution. Guided by resident scholarship and community leadership, the Center continues to grow as a beacon of learning, worship, and unity.

Our Objectives

Spiritual Growth & Worship

Facilitate regular spiritual observances, including daily prayers, Friday (Jumu'ah) prayers, supplications (Dua Kumayl, Dua Tawassul), and major Islamic commemorations throughout the year.

Educational Excellence

Provide high-quality academic and religious instruction through youth weekend programs, Quranic studies, adult seminars, and scholar-led lectures.

Youth & Family Support

Offer mentorship, recreational opportunities, and family counseling to support healthy homes and strong community bonds.

Community Outreach & Services

Provide essential religious services—including marriage ceremonies (Nikah), pastoral care, and community hall rentals—while building bridges with neighboring communities.
