قال الصادق علیه السلام
مَا مِنْ شَيْ‏ءٍ أَبْغَضَ إِلَى اللَّهِ عَزَّ وَ جَلَّ مِنَ الطَّلَاقِ. وسائل الشيعة، ج‏22، ص: 8

Imam Ja'far al-Sadiq (peace be upon him) said: 'There is nothing more hated by Allah, the Almighty and Majestic, than divorce.'

While Islam considers marriage to be a sacred contract built on affection, mercy, and mutual respect, life can present insurmountable challenges. In Islamic teachings, divorce (Talaq) is recognized as the most disliked of all permissible things. It is always viewed as a final recourse—an outcome to be avoided whenever possible through patience, effort, and understanding.

However, Islam is a practical and compassionate religion. When a marriage can no longer provide safety, peace, or mutual fulfillments, and all avenues of reconciliation have been exhausted, divorce becomes a dignified way out.

At the Imam Ali Center, we provide a safe, respectful, and confidential environment to guide you through this sensitive transition.
