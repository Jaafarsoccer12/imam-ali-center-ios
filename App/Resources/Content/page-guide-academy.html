Nurturing Mind, Character, and Faith

In today’s world, having an everyday full-time Islamic school is one of the most vital investments a community can make for its children. A full-time Islamic educational environment provides a safe, nurturing sanctuary where academic excellence and Islamic identity grow hand-in-hand every day. Rather than navigating conflicting values, students build deep-rooted confidence in their faith, practice daily prayers, internalize Islamic ethics (Akhlaq), and develop strong moral character alongside world-class academics.

We are thrilled to announce that Guide Academy is expanding to Northern Virginia and opening its new branch right here at the Imam Ali Center! Guide Academy is a premier, full-time Islamic educational institution fully accredited by the Western Association of Schools and Colleges (WASC). Guide Academy offers a unique, holistic educational path combining authentic Montessori education for early and primary years with the world-renowned International Baccalaureate (IB) Middle Years Program for higher grades. Through child-centered learning, hands-on exploration, critical thinking, and advanced academic standards, Guide Academy prepares students to excel in higher education while staying grounded in their Islamic heritage.
