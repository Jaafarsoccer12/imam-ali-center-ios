# Imam Ali Center iOS App Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

Imam Ali Center operates this public community information app. Contact us at iacdmv@gmail.com, (571) 444-3578, or 7900 Backlick Road, Springfield, VA 22150 with privacy questions or requests.

## App features and information on your device

The app does not require an account. Its native screens do not contain advertising or analytics SDKs and do not request your contacts, camera, microphone, photo library, or GPS location. Prayer times use a fixed location in Springfield, Virginia. The weekly schedule and selected reading pages are cached on your device. Removing the app removes these local app copies through the operating system.

Adding a gathering to your calendar opens Apple's event editor, where you review the event and choose whether to save it. The app does not upload your calendar contents to Imam Ali Center.

## Network requests and website content

The app requests current information from imamalicenter.org and prayer times from AlAdhan. Requests expose ordinary connection information, such as an IP address, to the provider receiving the request. The prayer-time request uses Springfield, Virginia, rather than your device's precise location.

Live information pages can load the Center's website inside the app. That website uses Google Analytics and Cloudflare services, and may load YouTube media and Google Maps. These services may process usage information, online identifiers, browser or device information, and connection logs, and may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies. The native app's lack of an analytics SDK does not mean that live web pages collect no data.

Apple Maps and other services opened by your actions are subject to those services' privacy policies. Social media, video, linked organizations, and donation providers also operate their own services. Review the relevant provider's policy before sharing information with it.

## Information you choose to send

If you contact the Center, sign up for updates, request a service, or make a donation, you may provide contact details, correspondence, or other information needed for that request. Contact forms that prepare an email allow you to review and send it through your email app. Payment details are handled by the payment provider; the native app does not store card details.

We use information you provide to respond to your requests, administer the services or updates you request, maintain necessary organizational records, and meet applicable obligations. We share information with service providers involved in those purposes or when required by law. Sensitive personal matters should be discussed directly with the Center rather than entered into an unrelated public website or social media service.

## Retention and privacy requests

The Center retains correspondence and service records only as long as reasonably needed to handle the request, administer the related service, maintain required records, or meet applicable obligations. Provider logs and analytics data follow the relevant provider's retention settings and policies. Contact iacdmv@gmail.com to request access, correction, or deletion of information you have sent to the Center, or to stop receiving optional updates. Some records may need to be retained for legal or operational obligations.

## Children and changes

The app provides public community information. Children should involve a parent or guardian before sending personal information or using external services. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact the Center so it can review the request.

We may update this policy as the app or services change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
